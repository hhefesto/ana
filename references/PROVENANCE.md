# Source Provenance

The implementation is original to this repository. The following local research
collections informed its specification but are not copied wholesale:

- `/home/hhefesto/src/conal-elliott`: Conal Elliott's work on denotational
  design, compiling to categories, automatic differentiation, and language
  derivatives.
- `/home/hhefesto/src/tai-danae-bradley`: Tai-Danae Bradley's work on enriched
  language categories, reduced densities, tensor networks, and magnitude.
- `/home/hhefesto/src/modArTransformer`: engineering reference for Agda/MAlonzo,
  Haskell conformance oracles, Futhark VJPs, and Nix integration.

`bradley/an-enriched-category-theory-of-languages-published.pdf` is copied from
`/home/hhefesto/Documents/tai-danae-bradley/an-enriched-category-theory-of-languages.pdf`.
It is the published Springer edition, SHA-256
`e13c1fd1c09301475d520a5485223d51b8f17a9d86cf7ad25896f3cab19f4bbf`, and is
different from the arXiv/source edition in the research collection.

The shorter markdown summary in `~/Documents/tai-danae-bradley` was not copied:
it overstates unrestricted composition, the density/Yoneda relationship, and
the connection to attention, and gives an incorrect magnitude formula.
